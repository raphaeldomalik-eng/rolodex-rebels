# Rolodex Rebels Strategy V2

**Status:** Controlling strategy for positioning, offer design and public implementation  
**Date:** 11 September 2026  
**Relationship to V1:** V1 remains the search-market evidence base. V2 supersedes it wherever the two documents conflict.

## 1. Strategic position

Rolodex Rebels is a specialist, technology-enabled music growth agency.

Publicly, that means a music marketing partner that joins up PR, discovery, audience growth, live demand, grassroots activation, digital experience and campaign learning. Technology supports the work; it is not the product, the headline or a seventh service pillar.

The organising promise remains:

> GET SEEN. GET HEARD. GET RESULTS.

The bridge between heritage and modern capability remains:

> FROM THE STREETS TO THE SCREENS.

The agency should feel independent, culturally fluent, commercially useful and operationally credible. It should never read like SaaS, an AI consultancy, a generic digital agency or an event-technology company.

## 2. Non-negotiable brand architecture

The public service architecture remains exactly six pillars:

1. **Get Heard** — music PR, campaign narrative, earned attention and connected follow-up.
2. **Get Seen** — relevant discovery across search, social, paid, creator and grassroots channels.
3. **Build Your Audience** — permission-based capture, understanding, communication, activation and retention.
4. **Sell The Show** — demand, response, ticket conversion, audience learning and repeat attendance.
5. **Grassroots** — street teams, flyer distribution, poster runs and place-based activation.
6. **Digital & Creative** — the digital infrastructure behind audience growth.

Do not add a Technology, Data, AI, Platform or Intelligence pillar. Do not expand the primary navigation into individual tactics or offer families.

## 3. Confidentiality and attribution rule

Internal experience may shape Rolodex Rebels methods, standards and capability. It must not create public attribution to private people, businesses, platforms, clients or projects.

> TRANSFER EXPERTISE. DO NOT TRANSFER ATTRIBUTION.

Public pages, metadata, structured data, case-study placeholders and sales language must not name or imply Raphael Domalik, Event Suite, Event Growth Studio, Allxs, Prestige ID, former companies, former brands, former artists, former clients or former projects unless separate written approval and substantiating evidence are supplied.

No private implementation history should be reverse-engineered into a public claim. No unverified artist, campaign, audience, ticket, venue or festival result should be published.

## 4. Market proposition

Rolodex Rebels helps independent artists, labels, managers, promoters, venues and festivals turn attention into lasting momentum.

The differentiator is integration: earned attention has somewhere useful to go; discovery connects to a relevant audience journey; live demand connects to ticket action and audience capture; grassroots activity connects audience, place and moment; digital experiences help people understand, act and stay connected; campaign response becomes learning for the next decision.

This is expressed through client outcomes, not tools: reach the right people; be easier to find and understand; build direct fan relationships; convert demand into meaningful action; learn what is working; and retain momentum between releases and shows.

## 5. Internal operating model

> DISCOVER → ENGAGE → CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → CONVERT → RETAIN → LEARN → GROW

This is an internal planning system, not a compulsory public diagram on every page.

- **Discover:** create relevant visibility through PR, search, social, paid, creators and place-based activity.
- **Engage:** give attention a strong story, idea, creative expression or moment worth responding to.
- **Capture:** where appropriate and permissioned, create a useful next step: sign-up, RSVP, follow, enquiry, ticket action or direct fan connection.
- **Understand:** read available signals across audience behaviour, geography, channel, creative, search, email, landing pages and sales response.
- **Convert:** reduce friction between interest and the most valuable next action.
- **Retain:** build an ongoing relationship rather than restarting the audience from zero every campaign.
- **Learn:** turn response into practical insight. Avoid dashboards without decisions.
- **Grow:** use learning, retained audiences and stronger infrastructure to improve the next release, show or campaign.

## 6. Technology principle

Technology should be invisible but powerful. Use plain English and lead with what the client can understand or do better. Mention audience signals, permission-based fan relationships, search visibility, ticket response, campaign learning, landing-page behaviour and useful reporting only where these help a decision.

Avoid public framing around stacks, platforms, proprietary systems, AI products, data products, martech or event tech. Never imply surveillance, perfect attribution or universal access to audience data.

Approved tone:

- “See what is getting a response and use it to make the next move stronger.”
- “Build direct, permission-based fan relationships.”
- “Make every campaign easier to find, act on and learn from.”
- “No data theatre. No jargon.”

## 7. Service strategy

### 7.1 Get Heard

**Role:** Make the story clear, credible and worth carrying.

PR remains central. The modern model connects positioning and storytelling to earned attention, content, creators and useful audience destinations.

> POSITION → STORY → EARN ATTENTION → DIRECT RESPONSE → FOLLOW UP → LEARN

Coverage is never guaranteed. Activity should be selective and relevant. Earned attention should lead somewhere useful where possible, and follow-up should strengthen the next release or campaign.

Offer families include Rebel Launch, Rebel Artist Growth, release campaigns, campaign storytelling, press activity, creator activity, content and fan communications.

### 7.2 Get Seen

**Role:** Create relevant discovery, not empty reach.

> Be found by people who matter — and give that attention somewhere useful to go.

> AUDIENCE → DISCOVERY → RELEVANCE → DESTINATION → RESPONSE → LEARNING

Capability can combine search and AI discovery readiness, paid media, social visibility, creator activity, content distribution and grassroots exposure. Never promise rankings, impressions, virality or guaranteed platform outcomes.

### 7.3 Build Your Audience

**Role:** Turn fragmented attention into direct, useful, permission-based fan relationships.

> STOP STARTING FROM ZERO.

> CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → COMMUNICATE → ACTIVATE → RETAIN → LEARN

Capability can include audience intelligence, Fan CRM, segmentation, fan acquisition, email, retargeting and retention. Public copy should describe useful outcomes: knowing who responded, communicating with relevance, activating demand and carrying learning forward.

No claim should imply that all fan data is available, that anonymous audiences can always be identified or that every channel permits direct retargeting.

Offer families include Rebel Artist Growth, audience growth programmes, fan relationship programmes and campaign intelligence.

### 7.4 Sell The Show

**Role:** Connect live demand to ticket action, audience understanding and future growth.

> DEMAND → RESPONSE → CONVERSION → AUDIENCE → RETENTION

> Sell more tickets is an outcome. It is not a channel strategy.

Where data is available, planning and optimisation can consider sales pace, time, geography, segments, channel response, creative response, email behaviour and landing-page behaviour. Availability must never be assumed.

For festivals and live programmes, the wider model is:

> BUILD DEMAND → SELL → CAPTURE → UNDERSTAND → RETAIN → GROW

Partnership thinking can support reach, relevance and audience value where genuinely useful, but there is no public partnership service page in this release.

Offer families include Rebel Tour, Promoter Growth, Festival Growth, ticket growth, tour marketing, RSVP campaigns and Guest & Industry Campaigns.

### 7.5 Grassroots

**Role:** Put the campaign in the real places and moments where audiences move.

Grassroots is a current strategic advantage, not a legacy footnote.

> AUDIENCE → PLACE → MOMENT → ACTIVATION → OPTIONAL DIGITAL RESPONSE → LEARNING

Capability includes street teams, leaflet drops, poster runs, hand-to-hand flyering, campus activity and local activation. Digital response mechanisms may be used where they improve the campaign, but must not be forced into every physical interaction.

Staffed Street Teams are offered only in London, surrounding areas and Kent. Do not describe Street Teams as nationwide. Do not fabricate GPS tracking, live field dashboards, route verification or distribution technology. Wider project geography may be explored for other services only when operationally credible and explicitly agreed.

### 7.6 Digital & Creative

**Role:** Provide the digital infrastructure behind audience growth.

> THE DIGITAL INFRASTRUCTURE BEHIND AUDIENCE GROWTH.

This pillar is broader than artist websites. It serves artists, bands, events, festivals, venues, promoters, organisations and campaigns through websites, campaign sites, microsites, landing pages, audience journeys, content infrastructure, search and AI discovery readiness, creative assets, measurement and managed improvement.

> AUDIENCE → NEED → EXPERIENCE → CONTENT → ACTION → RELATIONSHIP → LEARNING

Start with audience need, not a feature list. Create clear destinations for attention, reduce friction around the next valuable action, make content useful and discoverable, and measure what informs a decision.

> We can build it, run it and improve it with you.

This describes an available working model, not an implication that every project is fully managed.

## 8. Audience strategies

### Artists

Treat artist marketing as a growth journey, not a sequence of disconnected release pushes and never as artist management.

> Don’t just promote the next release; build momentum for what comes after.

> POSITION → AUDIENCE → DISCOVERY → RELEASE → CAPTURE → LIVE → COMMERCIAL OPPORTUNITY → RETAIN → GROW

Rolodex Rebels can coordinate the marketing across this journey. It does not represent, manage or make career decisions for the artist.

### Labels & Managers

The proposition is less fragmentation and more useful coordination. Rolodex Rebels acts as a specialist extension of the existing team, not a replacement for management, label functions or internal decision-making. Join PR, audience, digital, live and learning around a shared campaign objective.

### Promoters, Venues & Festivals

> BUILD DEMAND → SELL → CAPTURE → RETAIN → GROW

Campaigns can connect discovery, grassroots activation, ticket journeys, audience understanding and post-event retention. Do not imply access to data that promoters, ticketing partners or platforms have not made available.

**Results** remains paused until substantiated case studies are approved: hidden from primary navigation, `noindex`, excluded from the XML sitemap, no fictional proof, and no homepage CTA to the paused route. When activated, every case study follows CHALLENGE → WHAT ROLODEX REBELS DID → RESULT.

- Keep navigation and internal links crawlable without client-only workarounds.
