## Google baseline fields

Record by query and landing page:

- impressions
- clicks
- average position as a diagnostic field, not a promise
- query visibility and SERP features
- landing page
- enquiries
- conversion rate

## AI / LLM baseline fields

For each natural-language question, record separately for each assistant tested:

- whether Rolodex Rebels appears in the answer
- whether Rolodex Rebels is cited or linked
- wording and context of the mention
- sources cited by the answer
- identifiable referral sessions in analytics
- enquiries and conversions attributable to AI referrals

Recommended test surfaces: Google AI experiences where shown, ChatGPT, Microsoft Copilot, Perplexity and Gemini. Record date, model/product label where visible, locale and whether the session was signed in.

## Comparison cadence

- Capture the initial baseline after Search Console and Bing verification.
- Repeat monthly for the first three months after Insights and Results publishing begins.
- Review quarterly once visibility stabilises.
- Compare individual queries, citations, landing pages and conversions; do not collapse them into an invented score.
