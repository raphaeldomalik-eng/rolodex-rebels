## Verification and discovery mechanisms

- Google: `https://rolodexrebels.co.uk/google5545d78fd95057bb.html` returns the exact public verification response as HTML. The repository also supports an environment-provided Google metadata token.
- Bing: the site emits the existing `msvalidate.01` metadata value. Authenticated Bing Webmaster Tools was not accessed during this closeout.
- IndexNow: `https://rolodexrebels.co.uk/indexnow-key.txt` returns the public key as plain text. The protected submission endpoint accepts only URLs in the approved indexable route set; paused, unknown, query-string, fragment, and alternate-host URLs are rejected. `INDEXNOW_SECRET` is configured as a sensitive Vercel value for production and previews.
- AI-assisted search: `OAI-SearchBot` is explicitly allowed. Pages use ordinary crawlable HTML, descriptive headings, canonicals, internal links, and reliable schema; no `llms.txt`, hidden AI summaries, keyword blocks, or fake FAQs are used.
- Alternate hosts: `www` resolves permanently to the apex host. Vercel deployment aliases are protected and return `X-Robots-Tag: noindex`; production metadata never canonicalises to a Vercel hostname.

Authenticated Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools were not available in this execution context. Their account-level sitemap status, URL inspection reports, coverage reports, manual actions, and account ownership were therefore not claimed as verified.

## Internal linking policy

- Every indexable commercial page must receive at least one crawlable contextual link.
- Parent pillars link to their specialist child services; specialist pages link back to their parent and a small number of relevant adjacent services or audience routes.
- Festival Marketing and Event & Festival Websites link to each other where useful.
- Artists links to Music PR, Build Your Audience, Artist Website Design, and Sell The Show.
- Promoters, Venues & Festivals links to Festival Marketing, Event & Festival Websites, Sell The Show, Street Teams, and Flyer Distribution.
- Do not introduce footer keyword dumps or make every page link to every service.

## Conversion analytics and attribution

GA4 remains blocked until the visitor grants analytics consent. With consent, the site records the established conversion events: `start_project_cta`, `service_page_enquiry`, `audience_route_selection`, `project_form_started`, `project_form_submitted`, `phone_click`, and `email_click`.

The consented visit context includes the landing page, enquiry-origin page, latest relevant service page, latest relevant audience route, external referrer without its query string, and UTM source/medium/campaign/content/term. It is held in session storage for the browser tab, attached as context to conversion events, carried into hidden project-form fields, validated server-side, and included in the project-brief email. Rejecting analytics clears this attribution and empties the form fields. No attribution is sent with the form when analytics consent is denied.

## Closeout regression checks

- Crawl the sitemap and then all distinct internal links.
- Require 22 sitemap URLs until approved content or architecture changes are made.
- Require HTTP 200, index/follow, one same-path apex canonical, one H1, unique title and description, valid JSON-LD, and at least one inbound link for every indexable route.
- Require `/results` and `/insights` to remain noindex and outside navigation/sitemap while paused.
- Require unknown routes to return HTTP 404 and noindex.
- Require the 1200×630 default social image and complete Open Graph/X metadata.
- Test consent before accept, after accept, after reject, and across an internal navigation to `/start-a-project`.
- Test mobile at 375, 390, and 430 pixels and laptop/desktop at 1366 and 1440 pixels for readable content, usable navigation, and zero horizontal overflow.
