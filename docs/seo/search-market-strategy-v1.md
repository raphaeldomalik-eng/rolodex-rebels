## 1. Executive summary

Rolodex Rebels is positioned as a full-service specialist music marketing agency for artists, labels and managers, and promoters, venues and festivals. Its distinctive advantage is not a single channel. It is the ability to connect PR, digital visibility, audience relationships, live and ticket growth, and real-world grassroots activation in one campaign.

The existing commercial architecture remains the core of the site. It should not be replaced by dozens of thin keyword pages. Search growth should come from stronger proof, a small number of justified commercial landing pages, genuinely useful Insights, first-hand campaign evidence and relevant external authority.

The programme has three layers:

1. **Technical foundation — IMPLEMENTED.** Do not reopen without evidence of a genuine issue.
2. **Search-market strategy — RESEARCHED / READY FOR IMPLEMENTATION.** Keyword territories, intent, SERP patterns and URL ownership are defined below.
3. **Authority growth — NEXT MAJOR PHASE.** Results, original Insights, testimonials, approved client evidence, backlinks and industry references now matter more than further technical optimisation.

The strategic expression **FROM THE STREETS TO THE SCREENS** must remain central. Grassroots is a current differentiator and operating capability, not a legacy footnote. Event Suite and any underlying technology provider must not be mentioned publicly.

## 4. Search-market strategy

Search decisions must balance:

- commercial intent;
- relevance to real services;
- fit with the live SERP;
- realistic competitiveness;
- conversion value; and
- Rolodex Rebels' ability to demonstrate genuine credibility.

Volume alone is not a priority signal. A lower-volume query from someone ready to commission a street team, PR campaign or ticket-growth campaign may be more valuable than a broad high-volume informational query.

### Main SERP conclusions

#### Broad music marketing

Broad agency terms are competitive and authority-heavy. The homepage should continue to own broad music marketing agency intent without keyword stuffing. Progress will increasingly depend on proof, recognised work, backlinks, original content and industry references.

#### Music PR

Music PR has sufficiently distinct commercial intent to justify a dedicated future page. Until that page exists, `/services/get-heard` owns release marketing and the broad PR-related territory it can serve accurately.

#### Grassroots and Street Teams

Grassroots promotion and music Street Teams are among the strongest differentiated opportunities for Rolodex Rebels. They combine commercial intent, authentic operating experience and a proposition that many digital-only competitors cannot match. This is a strategic priority.

#### Live and ticket growth

`/services/sell-the-show` has strong commercial potential across concert, tour, promoter, venue and ticket-growth intent. The current limitation is proof, not positioning. Verified campaign and ticket outcomes are critical.

#### Artist website design

Music-specific artist and musician website design has distinct commercial intent and can justify a dedicated page if Rolodex Rebels intends it to remain a meaningful commercial service.

#### Audience growth

Audience and fan-growth searches contain substantial informational intent. Build authority through practical Insights and keep commercial ownership centred on `/services/build-your-audience` rather than creating many micro-service pages.

## 5. Geographic service rules

### Music Street Teams

**Locked operating area:** London, surrounding areas and Kent.

Rolodex Rebels must not describe staffed Street Team coverage as nationwide or UK-wide. The stable future URL is:

`/services/grassroots/music-street-teams`

Do not place London or Kent in the URL. The page should target London-led commercial demand while accurately stating the wider operating area.

Approved visible wording direction:

> Professional music and event street teams across London, surrounding areas and Kent.

Relevant search territory includes:

- music street teams London;
- street team agency London;
- street promotion London;
- concert street teams London;
- festival street teams London;
- music promotion street teams London;
- street teams Kent;
- music promotion Kent; and
- event street teams Kent.

Kent and surrounding-area wording should be natural, not repeated for keyword density. Do not create separate London and Kent pages now. A future geographic split requires all of the following:

- distinct demand in Search Console or equivalent reliable data;
- SERPs that show genuinely separate intent;
- unique local evidence from Rolodex Rebels; and
- pages that materially help users rather than acting as doorway pages.

Do not create Street Team pages for cities where staffed coverage does not exist.

Suggested title direction:

- `Music Street Teams London & Kent | Rolodex Rebels`; or
- `Music Street Teams London | Rolodex Rebels` if reliable title-length and SERP evidence supports the shorter form.

### All other services

Do not impose an artificial London-only boundary on PR, digital, audience, ticket, creative or other services. Organise them around service and audience intent. Do not create templated city pages such as `/music-pr-london`, `/music-pr-manchester` or `/festival-marketing-birmingham`.

## 7. Search-intent framework

Every opportunity must be classified before choosing or creating a page.

| Intent class | User behaviour | Typical buyer interpretation | Best response |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Transactional | Wants to commission or act now | Ready to hire | Focused commercial page and strong enquiry path |
| Commercial investigation | Comparing approaches or suppliers | Comparing agencies | Service/audience page with proof and differentiation |
| Informational | Seeking an answer or framework | Researching a solution | Experience-led Insight with natural service links |
| Navigational | Seeking Rolodex Rebels or a known page | Looking for a specific business/resource | Clear brand/entity result and direct route |
| Local | Needs physical execution in a real area | Looking for local physical delivery | Accurate coverage, logistics and local evidence |
| Mixed | SERP serves more than one plausible need | Planning a campaign or narrowing options | Use the dominant intent and support secondary intent without diluting the page |

Additional buyer interpretations to record are: looking for a specific service, planning a campaign, urgent problem solving and looking for local physical execution.

Search intent determines page type. Do not force informational and transactional intent into one page unless the live SERP demonstrates that a combined page is useful and competitive.

## 13. Cannibalisation rules

- Give each primary commercial intent one clear owning URL.
- Do not make multiple service pages target the exact same primary phrase without evidence that search engines separate the intents.
- Keep service pages focused on what is delivered; keep Who We Help pages focused on the audience's situation, goals and appropriate service mix.
- Use Insights to answer informational questions and support commercial owners rather than compete with them.
- Use Results to demonstrate an outcome and link to the relevant service and audience pages.
- Before creating a page, compare its proposed query set with every existing owner in this document.
- If two pages begin competing for the same query, first clarify intent and internal linking; consolidate when the distinction is not useful.
- Do not over-optimise internal anchors or repeat exact-match phrases mechanically.

1. Grassroots activation

**CHALLENGE → WHAT ROLODEX REBELS DID → RESULT**

Never invent attribution. When an older campaign has imperfect measurement, publish only what can be verified and state limitations plainly.

## 16. Internal-linking model

Use the primary relationship:

**INSIGHT → SERVICE → RESULT → START A PROJECT**

Links should help the reader take the next logical step. Use descriptive, natural anchors and avoid exact-match repetition.

## 17. AI/LLM discovery strategy

Retain the existing natural-language discovery baseline and test the buyer questions that best express real commercial need:

- Which UK agency can market an independent artist's new single?
- Who can run PR, digital and grassroots promotion for a music release?
- Which music marketing agency offers both on-the-ground and digital campaigns?
- Who can help a promoter sell more tickets?
- Who provides music street teams in London?
- Which agency can market a festival?
- Who can help an artist grow a real fan audience?
- Who can build an artist website and market the release?
- What does a full-service music marketing agency do?

Track separately:

- whether Rolodex Rebels appears;
- whether it is linked or cited;
- which competitors appear;
- which sources are cited;
- which external domains the answer relies upon;
- AI referral traffic; and
- enquiries attributable to AI discovery.

Do not invent a composite “AI ranking score”. First-hand campaign evidence, consistent entity information, authoritative external references and useful original answers are the core discovery strategy.

## 18. Off-site authority strategy

- artist and label website credits;

- campaign credits and partner pages;
- reputable agency and music directories;
- music-industry media, interviews and expert commentary;
- relevant guest contributions;

Avoid paid link farms, spam directories, automated backlink schemes, irrelevant guest-post networks, fake reviews and fabricated citations. Relevance and credibility matter more than link count.

The authority model is:

**REAL CLIENT WORK → RESULTS → ORIGINAL INSIGHTS → SEARCH / AI DISCOVERY → ENQUIRIES → MORE CLIENT WORK**

## 20. Measurement framework

### Early launch — weekly

Review only:

- indexing;
- crawl failures;
- sitemap issues; and
- critical technical problems.

### Monthly — first three to six months

Review:

- Google Search Console and Bing;
- commercial keyword visibility;
- landing-page impressions, clicks and CTR;
- enquiries and conversions;
- Insights and Results performance;
- AI referral traffic; and
- selected natural-language discovery questions.

### Quarterly

Review:

- keyword strategy and competitor changes;
- content gaps;
- authority and backlinks;
- possible page consolidation;
- evidence for new landing pages or locations; and
- changes in search intent.

Avoid daily rank obsession and do not make ranking guarantees.

## 21. Search Console / Bing optimisation loop

After Google Search Console DNS verification, record query-level data by landing page, audience and service pillar:

- query;
- landing page;
- impressions;
- clicks;
- CTR;
- average position as a diagnostic metric;
- conversions;
- audience; and
- service pillar.

Look especially for:

- high-impression, low-CTR opportunities;
- positions 4–20;
- unexpected queries;
- commercial long-tail queries;
- cannibalisation;
- pages gaining visibility; and
- pages losing visibility.

Use Bing as a secondary search signal. Decisions should be based on patterns and commercial outcomes, not isolated rank movements.
